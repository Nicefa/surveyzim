About Us — SurveyZim

Building Zimbabwe's data sovereignty, one survey at a time

SurveyZim exists to make sure Zimbabweans control, benefit from, and shape the data that describes our lives. We build tools, host data locally, and train people so that insights about Zimbabwe come from Zimbabwe not from servers and dashboards abroad.

Why Data Sovereignty Matters for Zimbabwe

For too long, external platforms have collected our stories, analysed them elsewhere, and captured the value. That leaves our researchers, policymakers and communities without the locally rooted intelligence they need.

Data sovereignty is about more than storage it is about ownership, trust, and the ability to turn insight into action inside our borders.

What We Do

  • Own our narrative — Capture authentic Zimbabwean perspectives without foreign interpretation.
  • Build local data assets — Create anonymized, reusable datasets that stay within Zimbabwe.
  • Develop indigenous analysis — Train and equip local researchers and data scientists.
  • Control our digital future — Ensure decisions about data use are made by Zimbabweans, for Zimbabweans.

Our Vision

A Zimbabwe where data strengthens communities, informs policy, and fuels homegrown innovation: local storage and processing of sensitive research data; clear, locally governed standards; and data-driven decisions that reflect Zimbabwe's realities.

How SurveyZim Contributes to National Progress

  • National research repository — Aggregated, anonymized survey data for the public good.
  • Stronger research capacity — Practical training and tools for students, researchers and civil society.
  • Better policy and service design — Evidence grounded in local context for health, education, agriculture and business.
  • Economic opportunity — Data assets that enable local innovation and investment.

Our Commitments

Built in Zimbabwe, for Zimbabwe

Technology and processes designed around local needs.

Local Hosting and Privacy

Sensitive research data is stored and processed within Zimbabwe where possible.

Capacity Building

Partnerships with universities, researchers and community organisations to grow skills.

Transparent Practice

Clear consent, anonymization and sharing policies that put communities first.

Who Benefits

Researchers

Get affordable, powerful tools that respect data sovereignty.

Communities

Receive clearer feedback, visible benefits and ethical handling of their information.

Policymakers and Businesses

Access locally relevant evidence to make better decisions.

Future Generations

Inherit a national data asset that supports innovation and resilience.

Using SurveyZim is an act of nation building. Every survey contributes to a shared resource that belongs to Zimbabwe, improving research quality, growing local expertise, and keeping our insights where they can do the most good.

This is our data. This is our story. This is our future.
